Research focus
What I work on
🔐

QKD Security Analysis

Finite-size effects, system imperfections, and protocol benchmarking in real-world quantum access and satellite networks.

🌐

Quantum Network Design

Wavelength assignment, quantum-classical coexistence over hollow-core fibre, and dynamic entanglement distribution.

⚛️

Quantum Repeater Networks

Blueprint architectures for scalable entanglement distribution and performance modelling for next-generation quantum networks.

🛡️

Post-Quantum Cryptography

Hybrid QKD + PQC security planes for optical transport networks and distributed quantum computing interconnects.
